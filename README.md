# Steam Oxide

**A fast, native Steam client for the Mac.**

Your library, store, friends and downloads in a lightweight app built for macOS,
with Windows games supported through CrossOver.

[**Download for macOS**](../../releases/latest)

</div>

## Features

- **Library**: browse, search and launch your games, organise them into collections
- **Store**: browse and search the Steam store, with wishlist
- **Friends and chat**: see who is online and what they are playing
- **Downloads**: fast, resumable installs and updates, with a clear queue
- **Workshop**: browse, subscribe and download Workshop items
- **Community**: read discussions, guides and broadcasts
- **Cloud saves**: your saves stay in sync, and conflicts are never overwritten silently
- **Windows games**: run them through CrossOver
- **Built for speed**: starts quickly and only loads what is on screen

## Requirements

- Mac with Apple silicon
- macOS 12 or later
- A Steam account
- CrossOver (optional, for Windows-only games)

## Install

1. Download `Steam-Oxide.dmg` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **Steam Oxide** into **Applications**.
3. On first launch, right-click the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
   The app is not notarized yet, so macOS asks once.
